01 / ORIGINS

The origins of MJX

MTR Japan Nexus (MJX) was created by bringing together the technologies, experience, and expertise developed independently by three communities in different fields.

Rather than simply merging existing worlds, MJX combines the strengths developed by each community: “railways,” “systems & technology,” and “architecture” into one new metropolitan area.

THREE COMMUNITIES
01 / RAILWAY

MJaS

MTR Japan Server

Opened around summer 2024. A large public MTR server operated and managed by MTR Japan Users.

With experience building and operating more than 800 stations and a scheduled rail network exceeding 70 km in total length, it contributes its know-how in constructing and operating large-scale railway lines, stations, and transport infrastructure to MJX.

02 / TECHNOLOGY

MTTS

New MTR Technology & Transport Server

Operated by an administration team including NEST.

A technology-focused community that has developed expertise in MTR-based railway construction, onboard digital signage and other systems, transport-related technologies and mechanisms, and server operation. It has also pursued railway construction with a different scale and direction from MJaS, as well as technical efforts to recreate modern Japanese transportation.

03 / ARCHITECTURE

JNC

JapanitureCraft

A community specializing in architecture, built around Japaniture Mod furniture and interior technology, with expertise in architecture, cityscapes, and interior design.

It contributes its skills and experience in creating realistic, high-quality buildings, interiors, and cityscapes to MJX.

Three Strengths, One City

  • MJaS — Expertise in large-scale railway lines and transport infrastructure construction
  • MTTS — Expertise in transportation systems, technology, and railway construction
  • JNC — Expertise in architecture, cityscapes, and interior design

By combining these three strengths, MJX aims to be a Minecraft community where players can enjoy an entire city—not only railways or architecture.

MJX Concept & Purpose

MTR Japan Nexus (hereinafter “MJX”)is a community server that combines MJaS’s railway network, MTTS’s advanced technology and transport infrastructure, and JNC’s realistic architecture and cityscapes to create a single world in Minecraft where urban life, transportation, daily life, and an economy come together.

Its purpose is for players to develop the metropolitan area together through railways, vehicles, construction, store management, events, and other activities.

While respecting freedom of activity, MJX places strong emphasis on protecting other players' creations, property, facilities, and the server environment.

02 / NEXT CHAPTER

Final Note

MJX is an integrated city server that brings together MJaS’s “railway infrastructure,” MTTS’s “advanced transportation technology,” and JNC’s “furniture and scenic design.”
Builders create the city, while Visitors visit, work, shop, and use transportation.
Let's grow one metropolitan area together—both those who build it and those who live in it!
