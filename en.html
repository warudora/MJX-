🔗 About MJX (Combining the Expertise of Three Predecessor Communities)

MTR Japan Nexus (MJX)was created by bringing together the technologies, experience, and expertise developed independently by three communities in different fields.

Rather than simply merging existing worlds, MJX combines the strengths developed by each community: “railways,” “systems & technology,” and “architecture” into one new metropolitan area.

MJaS

MTR Japan Server

Opened around summer 2024. A large public MTR server operated and managed by MTR Japan Users.

With experience building and operating more than 800 stations and a scheduled rail network exceeding 70 km in total length, it contributes its know-how in constructing and operating large-scale railway lines, stations, and transport infrastructure to MJX.

MTTS

New MTR Technology & Transport Server

Operated by an administration team including NEST.

A technology-focused community that has developed expertise in MTR-based railway construction, onboard digital signage and other systems, transport-related technologies and mechanisms, and server operation. It has also pursued railway construction with a different scale and direction from MJaS, as well as technical efforts to recreate modern Japanese transportation.

JNC

JapanitureCraft

A community specializing in architecture, built around Japaniture Mod furniture and interior technology, with expertise in architecture, cityscapes, and interior design.

It contributes its skills and experience in creating realistic, high-quality buildings, interiors, and cityscapes to MJX.

🌐 Three Strengths, One City

  • MJaS — Expertise in large-scale railway lines and transport infrastructure construction
  • MTTS — Expertise in transportation systems, technology, and railway construction
  • JNC — Expertise in architecture, cityscapes, and interior design

By combining these three strengths, MJX aims to be a Minecraft community where players can enjoy an entire city—not only railways or architecture.

[MJX Concept & Purpose]

MTR Japan Nexus (hereinafter “MJX”)is a community server that combines MJaS’s railway network, MTTS’s advanced technology and transport infrastructure, and JNC’s realistic architecture and cityscapes to create a single world in Minecraft where urban life, transportation, daily life, and an economy come together.

Its purpose is for players to develop the metropolitan area together through railways, vehicles, construction, store management, events, and other activities.

While respecting freedom of activity, MJX places strong emphasis on protecting other players' creations, property, facilities, and the server environment.

1. 🌐 General Rules (Applies to All Players)

These are the basic rules that apply to all players participating in MJX, regardless of whether they are Builders or Visitors.

1-1. Basic Conduct & Prohibited Actions

🚫 Respect for Other Players' Builds & Property

The following items created, owned, or managed by other players may not be destroyed, moved, used, or taken without permission.

  • Buildings
  • Roads & railway lines
  • Vehicles
  • Items & chests
  • Vending machines & shops
  • Other installed facilities

Intentional or malicious destruction, theft, or griefing may result in strict action, including an immediate ban.
For accidental damage, the administration will respond after reviewing the circumstances and extent of the damage.

⚔️ Chaos Elements & Combat (TacZ, etc.)

Indiscriminate player killing (PK), gunfire, explosions, or other combat in the city that involves other players' buildings, vehicles, facilities, or similar property is prohibited.

Test firing, combat, and filming using weapons such as TacZ must be conducted in designated test ranges, filming areas, PvP events, or other designated locations.

When specifically permitted by an event or similar activity, that event's rules take priority.

⚡ Server Load Considerations

To maintain stable server operation, avoid activities that generate excessive load, such as the following:

  • Continuous operation of large numbers of clock circuits
  • Excessive Create or similar power and rotational mechanisms
  • Leaving large numbers of entities unattended
  • Leaving large numbers of vehicles unattended
  • Leaving large numbers of items or dropped items unattended
  • Other facilities or devices that place a significant load on server operation

If the administration determines that a facility places excessive load on the server, it may request that the facility be stopped, removed, modified, or otherwise adjusted.

1-2. 💰 Economy System (Lightman's Currency)

MJX uses coins as its in-server currency.

🏦 Banking & Infrastructure

The following financial services are available through ATMs, banks, and similar facilities in the city.

  • Deposits
  • Withdrawals
  • Account-to-account transfers
  • Other supported financial services

🪙 Earning Currency

Visitors can primarily earn coins through activities such as the following.

  • Work commissioned by Builders or the administration
  • Stores & services
  • Driving taxis, buses, and similar vehicles
  • Selling products, food, and similar goods
  • Operating vending machines
  • Participating in events and competitions
  • Other approved work or services

🛒 Spending Currency

Coins can be used for the following services and products.

  • MTR and other fares / commuter passes
  • Expressway tolls
  • Vehicle purchases
  • Weapons & ammunition
  • Furniture, appliances & interiors
  • Restaurants & shops
  • Property rental & purchase
  • Other supported products and services

📉 Inflation Prevention

To maintain a healthy economy, currency is removed from circulation through measures such as the following.

  • Fares
  • Expressway tolls
  • Accident repair costs
  • Administrative fines
  • Various fees
  • Other economic adjustments

The administration may adjust prices, fees, and similar charges according to the state of the in-server economy.

1-3. 🚗 Road Traffic & Accident Response (MTS / Kaminari, etc.)

🚨 Responsibilities & Compensation After an Accident

If you cause an accident while driving a vehicle and damage a building, road, vehicle, facility, or similar property, immediately ensure safety, do not leave the accident unaddressed, and promptly report it to the parties involved or the administration.

[Restoration & Compensation]
The administration will determine the restoration method, repair costs, and similar matters based on the extent of the damage. As a general principle, discussion between the parties will be respected, with the administration mediating when necessary.

🚫 Prohibition of Malicious Failure to Address Accidents

As a general principle, causing an accident itself does not immediately result in severe disciplinary action.

However, malicious conduct such as the following may be subject to disciplinary action.

  • Hit-and-run
  • Intentional accidents
  • Concealing accidents
  • False reports
  • Intentionally leaving an accident unaddressed
  • Repeatedly engaging in similar conduct
1-4. 📹 Streaming & Recording

Streaming, video recording, and photo posting on YouTube, Twitch, and similar platforms are very welcome!

If other players appear in your content, please respect their privacy and playing environment.

Recommended hashtags: #MJXServer #MTRJapanNexus
1-5. ⚠️ Server & Mod Environment Disclaimer

Because MJX uses many mods and plugins, the following may occur:

  • Unscheduled maintenance
  • Specification changes
  • Issues caused by mods, plugins, and similar components
  • World restoration or rollback
  • Data loss
  • Other unexpected failures

If data loss or similar incidents occur, complete recovery cannot be guaranteed.

For situations not explicitly covered by these rules, the administration will make the final determination after reviewing the circumstances.

1-6. 🌐 Language & Interpretation of the Rules

The Japanese version of the MJX rules is the original text and the official standard.

The English version and other language versions are translations intended to help readers understand the Japanese version and do not replace it.

If differences in wording or nuance in a translation result in an interpretation that differs from the Japanese version, the Japanese wording and the official interpretation provided by the administration take priority.

If you have questions about the rules or are unsure how to interpret them, do not make an independent determination based solely on a translated version; ask the administration for clarification.

Once the administration has provided an explanation or correction, the interpretation provided by the administration will thereafter be treated as the official interpretation within MJX.

If, even after an explanation or correction from the administration, a participant repeatedly insists on or applies a different interpretation and interferes with the enforcement of the rules, the first occurrence will result in a notice or warning. If the behavior does not improve, it may be subject to disciplinary action, including suspension of permissions or a temporary ban.

However, for misunderstandings without malicious intent, such as simple translation errors or misinterpretation of the content, the administration will first provide an explanation or correction.

2. 🔨 Builder (Construction & Development) Rules

Builders are a participation category for developers who carry out urban development, construction, transportation, infrastructure development, and similar activities in MJX.

🏅 2-1. Builder Eligibility

Applicants must undergo the designated Builder selection process by the MTR Global Server Alliance (MGSA) and meet the required conditions.

After passing the selection process, permissions to conduct urban development, construction, and similar activities in MJX will be granted.

🏗️ 2-2. Building & Development Permissions

Builders are granted Creative permissions and may freely carry out urban development without applying for land acquisition.

However, changing or removing another person's existing buildings, land, facilities, transportation infrastructure, or similar property without permission is prohibited.

🏗️ 2-3. Building List & Schematic Sharing

Created buildings are shared as Schematics through a "Building List" that other Builders can use for urban development.

[Building Registration Procedure]
Post in the Discord “Building List” channel using the following format.
Building Name + Finished Photo
Example: 🏪 Convenience Store + 📷 Finished Photo

After that, save the Schematic on the Minecraft server.
Use the building name as the file name.
Example: Convenience Store.schem

[Registration & Usage Permission]

By registering a building in the "Building List," the creator is considered to have granted the following permission.

Builders within MJX may freely install and use the Schematic for the purpose of urban development.

No individual permission is required for each installation. The same Schematic may also be installed in multiple locations when necessary for urban development.

[Unregistered Buildings]

For buildings not registered in the "Building List," do not use or install their Schematics without the creator's permission.

[Building Modifications]

Exterior, interior, equipment, and similar elements may be modified to fit the installation location and cityscape.

However, avoid major modifications that significantly compromise the original creator’s artistic intent, or treating a modified building as the original creator’s work. If a substantially modified building is re-registered as a new building, identify the original creator whenever possible.

[Removal from Registration]

The creator may request the administration to stop new use of the Schematic.

However, as a general principle, buildings already installed or used before removal from registration, as well as Schematics already obtained or placed, will not be removed or recovered.

🚆 2-4. Railways & Infrastructure (MTR Mod)

Builders may construct railway lines without prior application.

Overlapping routes and parallel lines are also generally permitted.

A railway line will not be immediately stripped of its rights or forcibly removed solely because development has been inactive for a long period.

However, changing or removing another player’s buildings, facilities, existing infrastructure, or similar property without permission is prohibited.

⚙️ 2-5. Use of Create Mod

Create Mod materials, decorations, functions, and similar features may be used freely.

However, facilities that place excessive load on the server, such as the following, may be prohibited or restricted:

  • Large rotational mechanisms
  • Large numbers of clock circuits
  • Facilities using large numbers of entities
  • Other facilities that place a significant load on server operation

As a general principle, MTR Mod is used as the main railway system.

💰 2-6. Builder Economic Participation Rules

[Prohibition of Unlimited Distribution of Creative Resources]

Builders may not directly distribute unlimited quantities of items, building materials, equipment, or similar resources generated through Creative mode to Visitors or others.

To maintain the economic balance of MJX, Builders are also prohibited from directly distributing unlimited quantities of their coins to Visitors or others.

[Recommended Contract Work & Official Businesses]

Hiring Visitors for work and paying them in coins is encouraged.

Examples: guides, drivers, filming, construction work, store staff, and other work

Operating official stores or businesses approved by the administration is also encouraged.

Examples: showrooms, vehicle dealerships, restaurants, shops, and other service businesses

3. 🚶‍♂️ Visitor (General Participant & Resident) Rules

Visitors are a participation category for general players who enjoy sightseeing, shopping, transportation, work, daily life, events, and similar activities in MJX's cities.

🎮 3-1. Participation Environment

Visitors generally participate in Adventure Mode.

Therefore, during normal play, Visitors cannot destroy blocks or modify city facilities.

Upon joining, Visitors receive an initial allowance from the administration.

💼 3-2. Ways to Earn Money

Coins can be earned through activities such as the following.

  • Customer service
  • Driving
  • Guiding
  • Contract work
  • Store management
  • Product sales
  • Vending machine operation
  • Participating in events and competitions
  • Other approved work or services
🛒 3-3. Ways to Spend Money
  • Transportation: MTR fares, commuter passes, and Expressway tolls
  • Mobility: Vehicles, dealers, and MTS / Kaminari-related products
  • Equipment: Weapons & ammunition such as TacZ, and other supported equipment
    * Follow MJX’s combat and safety rules when using weapons & ammunition.
  • Daily life: JNC-style furniture and appliances, food and dining, shop products, property purchases and rentals, and other supported products and services
4. 🏙️ How to Play
  • 🔨 Builders: Freely develop cities, homes, stations, roads, railways, stores, and various facilities.
  • 🚶 Visitors: Walk around the city, use transportation, and live in the city through shopping, work, events, and other activities.
  • 🚆 Transportation: Travel around the city by riding MTR or driving vehicles yourself.
  • 💰 Economy: Earn coins through work, stock products, and operate shops and vending machines.
  • 📹 Content Creation: Share city life, construction, transportation, events, and other content through streams, videos, photos, and similar media.
5. ⚖️ Administrative Actions

If a violation of the rules is confirmed, the administration may take the following actions after considering the nature and severity of the violation, the extent of damage, previous violations, and other relevant circumstances.

  • Notice / Warning
  • Request to modify buildings, facilities, or similar property
  • Recovery of items, currency, or similar assets
  • Temporary suspension of permissions
  • Suspension or revocation of Builder permissions
  • Temporary ban
  • Permanent ban
The following conduct will be handled particularly strictly:

Griefing, theft, intentional destruction, attacks intended to overload the server, fraudulent manipulation of the economy, and other conduct that seriously interferes with server operation
