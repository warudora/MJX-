MJX Cinema System Policy

MTR Japan Nexus（MJX）

Article 1 — Purpose

This policy sets forth rules for safe and appropriate use of cinema systems in MJX servers.

This system may handle video and audio content related to external services such as YouTube.

Article 2 — Available Content

General users can use the following contents as a general rule.

1. Video and audio created by the user for which the user holds the necessary rights

2. Content for which the necessary permission has been obtained from the rights holder

3. Content that may be screened or distributed within the scope of its terms of use

4. Content approved by MJX operation

Note: Public availability on YouTube does not mean that redistribution or screening within MJX is permitted.

Article 3 — Prohibitions

The following acts are prohibited:

1. Screening or redistributing movies, anime, paid streaming works, or similar content without the copyright holder’s permission

2. Use of illegally uploaded content

3. Circumventing access restrictions on paid services or copy-protection measures

4. Play video and audio for the purpose of annoying other users

5. Acts that cause excessive load on the system

6. Other acts that violate laws, service terms, and MJX common rules

Article 4 — YouTube

When using YouTube content, comply with YouTube’s terms of service and the terms of use set by the rights holders.

Even if this system can technically play content, that does not necessarily mean its use is permitted.

Regarding the acquisition, storage, screening, and redistribution of external content, we will confirm the necessary rights and licensing.

Article 5 — Management

MJX operation may use different management functions than general users for system maintenance, operation confirmation, content management and event management.

We will comply with relevant laws, copyrights and external service terms and conditions for the handling of content by administrators.

Article 6 — Response to violations

In the event of a violation of this Policy, the MJX operation may take the following measures:

• Stop or delete the content

• Restrictions on use of the cinema system

• Stop permissions on the server

• Suspension of access to the server in serious cases

Article 7 — Disclaimer

Please note that MJX operation may not be able to correctly play video and audio due to external service specifications, communication failure, system failure, etc.

This policy does not exempt from liability that cannot be exempted by law.

Article 8 — Policy Revisions

This policy may be revised in response to changes to system functions, laws, or the terms of external services.

Established: October 11, 2026

Operator: MTR Japan Nexus (MJX)

MTR Japan Nexus — General Server Regulations

October 05, 2026

Preamble

These Regulations establish common rules for using services operated by MTR Japan Nexus (hereinafter “MJX”).

In these Regulations, “the Services” means the Discord server, Minecraft server, and other related services operated by MJX.

Additional rules established separately for each service also apply.

Article 1 — Legal Compliance

The User shall comply with laws and regulations in Japan and local laws and regulations in which the User resides.

Article 2 — Language

1. This service is based on the use of Japanese and English.

2. The user must strive for smooth communication with other users.

3. Management may designate the language to be used in particular services or channels when necessary.

Article 3 — Prohibition of Harassment and Nuisance

Users shall not conduct the following acts:

1. Annoyance to other users, intimidating behavior, excessive provocative behavior

2. Defamation, abusive language, insults, and discriminatory statements

3. Acts that unfairly disparage other users or organizations

4. Political or religious statements, discussions, solicitation, or other conduct that management deems inappropriate

5. Posting content against public order and morals, adult or excessive sexual content

6. Acts that interfere with other users' activities

7. Other acts that the operation deems to be annoying or improper act

Even if the person had no malicious intent, disciplinary measures may be imposed depending on the nature and effects of the conduct.

Article 4 — Handling of Personal Information

1. Users must not publish another user’s personal information without that person’s permission.

2. Please be careful when publishing your personal information.

3. Collecting, publishing, spreading, or otherwise handling personal information in ways management deems inappropriate may result in disciplinary action.

Article 5 — Prohibition of spam and dangerous content

1. Spam, excessive repeated posting, and mass posting of meaningless messages are prohibited.

2. Posting viruses, malware, or files and links that may harm users or the services is prohibited.

3. Posting links or content equivalent to R15 or R18 material is prohibited. MJX management may delete content or impose measures when it considers the material dangerous or inappropriate, regardless of the destination linked.

Article 6 — Prohibition of Unauthorized Advertising and Solicitation

Do not engage in commercial advertising, advertising, solicitation to other servers, or any other similar acts without the permission of the operation.

Article 7 — Handling of Related Organizations and Other Servers

1. Discussion of the MTR Global Server Alliance (MGSA), other affiliated organizations, and other Minecraft servers should generally take place in locations designated by management.

2. As a rule, MJX does not get involved in disputes that arise on other servers. This does not apply to matters involving MGSA.

3. When management judges that activities involving MGSA significantly affect MJX operations, those activities may be subject to measures even if they occurred outside MJX.

4. When necessary, MJX may share information about users’ violations and similar matters with MGSA and other related organizations.

5. When a governing decision is made to impose an MGSA-wide ban on a particular user, MGSA member servers, including MJX, must comply by suspending that user’s access or taking other required measures.

6. Measures under the preceding paragraph are based on the MGSA-wide ban decision; individual member servers do not independently decide whether that decision applies.

7. Management may consider information about disciplinary actions by MGSA or related organizations when necessary. Except for MGSA-wide bans, such information alone does not automatically result in disciplinary action within MJX.

Article 8 — Use of Multiple Accounts

1. Users may use the Service using multiple accounts (including sub-accounts).

2. Harassment, evasion of disciplinary action, and other inappropriate acts using multiple accounts are prohibited.

3. Operations may take measures such as restriction of use if multiple accounts are considered inappropriate.

Article 9 — Other Inappropriate Conduct

Even if conduct is not explicitly listed in these Regulations, management may take necessary measures if it determines that the conduct disrupts MJX operations, interactions among users, or activities with related organizations.

Article 10 — Application of These Rules to Administrators

1. In principle, the operator must comply with this provision.

2. However, if there is the approval of the highest administrator, the operator can act in conflict with this rule.

3. The Supreme Administrator may exempt all or part of the provisions of the Terms and Conditions at his/her own discretion, or act in conflict with the provisions.

Article 11 — Response to Violations and Disciplinary Measures

1. If the user violates this rule, the operation may take the following measures according to the content and degree of violation.

• Caution • Warning • Timeout • Temporary suspension of access • Permanent suspension of access

2. Management determines the type and duration of disciplinary measures in light of the violation’s nature, circumstances, and effects.

3. Management may combine multiple measures when necessary.

4. Disciplinary measures may be imposed without notifying the offender in advance.

5. Measures following a formally decided MGSA-wide ban are governed by Article 7.

Article 12 — Appeals

1. Users may request an explanation of disciplinary measures from management.

2. As a rule, filing an appeal does not suspend the effect of a disciplinary measure.

3. Management may amend or withdraw a disciplinary measure after reviewing an appeal if it considers doing so necessary.

4. MJX may not alter or withdraw the decision on the MGSA-wide BAN.

Article 13 — Automatic detection system, etc.

1. This service may use automatic detection systems such as Wick.

2. Users may request management to review disciplinary measures resulting from automated detection.

3. If a false detection is confirmed, management will modify or withdraw the measure as necessary.

4. Restrictions may be imposed depending on the circumstances, even when account hijacking or other acts occurred without the user’s intent.

Article 14 — Reviewing the Rules

When using the Services, users must review and comply with these Regulations and any other rules established by management.

Article 15 — Revisions to the Regulations

1. Management may revise these Regulations when necessary.

2. In principle, revisions may be made without prior notice.

3. The revised regulations shall apply from the time of publication.

Article 16 — Rules for Individual Services

1. Minecraft servers and other individual services are subject to their own separately established regulations in addition to these Regulations.

2. Where service-specific regulations differ from these Regulations, management determines which rules apply according to the service or conduct concerned.

3. The User shall comply with the provisions of this Agreement and the individual provisions of each Service.

Supplementary Provisions

1. This provision shall be enforced on October 05, 2026.

2. Management decides matters not covered by these Regulations when necessary.

3. Management decides any questions concerning the interpretation of these Regulations.

MTR Japan Nexus Builder

Established October 26, 2026

MTR Japan Nexus

Preamble

These Regulations establish the qualifications, authority, duties, and administrative procedures of Builders in MTR Japan Nexus (MJX).

A Builder is a person who performs construction, urban development, or maintenance of rail and transport infrastructure within MJX.

MJX respects Builder’s free development activities and protects participants’ rights, existing facilities, server environments and economic systems.

These Regulations apply together with the MJX General Server Regulations and other related regulations.

Chapter 1 — General Provisions

Article 1 — Purpose

1. This provision aims to balance Builder’s free and continuous development activities and MJX benefits.

2. Builder will comply with these regulations and related regulations and work with other participants to develop MJX.

3. This provision does not limit the development activities of Builder without a legitimate reason.

Article 2 — Builder Qualifications and Selection

1. Builder qualifications are obtained through the MTR Global Server Alliance (MGSA) selection system, or through the procedures approved by MJX.

2. Qualifications, selection methods and activity rights are subject to the MGSA's common system and MJX related regulations.

3. If MJX introduces its own selection procedures or powers, it must separately specify their details and conditions of application. MJX must not modify MGSA’s system beyond its own authority.

4. If there is a violation of the regulations after obtaining the qualification, it is subject to the measures based on Article 19.

Article 3 — Fundamental Builder Permissions

1. Builder can develop buildings, roads, railway lines, stations, traffic facilities and stores within MJX.

2. Builders are granted the permissions needed for development according to their qualification category.

3. Pre-application of land acquisition is not required and free development is permitted. However, restriction based on the rights and related regulations of others applies.

4. Builders must not improperly obstruct another person’s development or modify, move, remove, or destroy existing buildings, equipment, or property without permission.

5. Operations can limit development within a reasonable and necessary range for safety, public needs and protection of other rights.

Article 4 — ACTIVITIES

Builder must adhere to:

1. Respect the architecture, property, facilities and development activities of others.

2. Make reasonable efforts not to place an excessive load on the server.

3. Cooperate to the extent reasonably necessary when management requests reasonable assistance with investigating violations, modifying equipment, or reducing system load.

4. If there is a problem with our activities, we will promptly check the situation and report it to the operation as necessary.

Article 5 — Relationship with MJX Management

1. The general decision about Builder is made by the general administrator or other administrator.

2. Each person’s permissions are subject to MJX’s rules and permissions.

3. Final decision-making on the entire MJX is subject to the highest management regulations.

4. The operation shall be determined in consideration of the relevant provisions, facts, fairness, and the impact on the entire MJX.

5. As a rule, management explains the reasons for measures that affect a Builder’s rights or activities.

Chapter 2 — Development and rights management

Article 6 — Priority Rights of Use

1. Builder can have priority ownership rights for specific areas, buildings, routes, etc. by approval of operation.

2. Priority holding rights are intended to coordinate development among Builders. They do not constitute unlimited land ownership or blanket authority to prohibit legitimate development by others.

3. Applications for priority holding rights must specify the covered area, administrator, and development purpose. Approved rights are recorded and managed by MJX management.

4. Specific criteria such as the scope, number of cases, application and approval method that can be held are determined separately by the operation.

5. Maintain the principle of no prior application required in Article 3 for normal development without priority holding rights.

6. When competing with other Builder developments, prioritize consultations between parties, and if not resolved, the operation will adjust in consideration of existing rights, development status, and publicity.

7. Holders should continue developing and managing their areas. Progress includes construction, specific renovation, and substantive design-based work; a declaration of ownership or an announced plan alone normally does not count.

8. Rights generally remain in effect during an approved leave. However, where they seriously obstruct others’ development or public needs require action, changes or cancellation may be considered after notifying the holder and reviewing the circumstances.

9. As a rule, prior notice and an opportunity to explain or improve must be provided before changes or cancellation of rights are considered.

10. In cases of serious violations or emergency safety needs, management may temporarily restrict rights and must explain the reasons to the person concerned as soon as reasonably possible.

Article 7 — Development Areas and Others’ Works

1. Builder respects areas, buildings, facilities, and traffic infrastructure developed and managed by others.

2. In the event of change, movement, removal, or modification of the outcomes of others, in principle, obtain permission of the right holder.

3. If the right holder is unclear or uncontactable, consult the operation.

4. In order to ensure safety and emergency response to serious obstacles, we can carry out only the necessary range at the discretion of the operation.

5. Disputes related to rights shall be subject to Article 20.

Article 8 — Identification of Owners

1. Builder installs signs that show owners and administrators for buildings, routes and facilities as necessary.

2. The display uses information that can be used to identify the management requirements such as the Builder name and organization name.

3. For those who need to check public facilities or administrators, you can ask for indications for reasonable reasons.

4. Rights are not lost solely because an owner-identification sign is absent.

5. If the owner is unclear, the operation can be determined by confirming the record, installation history, and surrounding situation.

Article 9 — Registration and Sharing of Schematics

1. Builder can be registered as Schematic, such as architectures created by himself, or architectures that have the necessary permission to license others.

2. Registration is completed by posting the building’s name, completion photos, creator name, and other required details in the designated “Building List” on Discord or a similar platform. The saved filename should generally match the registered name.

3. The registrant must have the authority to grant permission to install, duplicate, modify, and other Builder in MJX for the registration.

4. A registered Schematic is deemed licensed for other Builders to use for urban development within MJX, within the conditions stated at registration.

5. As a rule, the permission in the preceding paragraph includes the following acts, and individual permission is not required for each installation.

• Installation and use within MJX • Installation at multiple locations • Modification of exteriors, interiors, and equipment to suit the surroundings or intended use

6. Any additional conditions or restrictions must be stated at registration so other Builders can check them before use. Conditions that conflict with these Regulations require management review.

7. Unregistered Schematic should not be used or installed without the author or author’s permission.

8. Please do not register any data without permission to register or reuse the work of others.

9. In the case of re-registration as a separate building, in principle, specify the original creator. However, if there is a legitimate reason, you can discuss with the operation. Do not display falsely about the original creator.

10. The display of creators in normal installation and minor modification is optional unless otherwise specified at the time of registration.

11. Registered users can cancel their registration and stop new use in the future.

12. As a rule, buildings placed before deregistration, and Schematics already acquired or placed, are not removed or reclaimed. However, new buildings generally may not be placed after deregistration, even if the user already possesses the data.

13. For development projects in progress when registration is withdrawn, management decides whether work may continue, taking progress and impacts on those concerned into account.

14. In the event of a violation of rights or serious regulations, the operation can take necessary measures such as suspension or removal.

15. The licensing of this Article does not grant the right to which the registrant does not have, and does not infringe the rights of laws and third parties.

Article 10 — Create Mod and Technical Equipment

1. Builders may use the Create Mod and similar tools according to MJX’s Mod environment and related regulations.

2. Technical equipment must not significantly disrupt server stability or the activities of other participants.

3. Facilities with large loads such as power generation and transmission mechanisms are subject to the prohibited and permission lists established by the operation.

4. The list must specify the covered blocks, mechanisms, and conditions of use, and may be revised to reflect Mod updates.

5. The adoption or amendment of a list must generally be announced in advance along with its effective date. Unannounced restrictions may not be applied retroactively, except for violations of existing rules or emergency safety measures.

6. If excessive load or malfunctions are confirmed, management may require the equipment concerned to be stopped, modified, or removed.

7. You can temporarily restrict it in case of emergency, but please explain the reason as soon as possible.

8. In principle, MTR is used as the main system for railway construction.

Chapter 3 — Economic activities and prohibited matters

Article 11 — Distribution of Resources and Coins

1. Builders must not directly distribute unlimited quantities of items, construction materials, equipment, or other resources generated in Creative mode to Visitors or others.

2. To maintain MJX’s economic balance, Builders must not distribute unlimited coins directly or improperly bypass the economic system to distribute coins.

3. Legitimate payment for work, rewards officially recognized by management, event prizes, and materials needed for joint development are not prohibited across the board.

4. Exceptions in the preceding paragraph remain subject to the Economic Regulations and other related rules and do not permit unconditional distribution.

5. Activities with a major economic impact, such as supplying materials to new participants or distributing materials in bulk or continuously, are subject to approval criteria separately established by management.

6. If it is unclear whether distribution is permitted or what its limit is, check with management in advance.

7. If improper distribution or economic manipulation is discovered, management may take necessary measures, including recovering items and coins, and request distribution records and an explanation of the circumstances.

Article 12 — Store Operations and Contracting

1. Builder can commission information, driving, shooting, architecture, shop staff, etc. to other participants and pay compensation by coin.

2. Business activities such as stores and services are recommended by official stores and businesses that are authorized to operate in with economic regulations and other related regulations.

3. Ads and solicitations are subject to Article 6 of the MJX Common Regulations, and adverts that require permission will be approved in advance.

4. Transactions involving real money or other value outside the game are subject to separately established rules. Any required approval must be obtained in advance.

5. As a rule, the parties must agree in advance on compensation, deadlines, scope of work, and other terms of outsourced tasks.

6. Disputes for consignment shall be subject to Article 20.

Article 13 — Confidential Information

1. Builders must not disclose nonpublic information learned through their activities to third parties without legitimate grounds.

2. Confidential information includes private personal information, authentication information, unpublished management decisions, security information, etc.

3. If there is a formal permission of the operation, it is necessary to report and investigate the violation of the provisions, if necessary by law or legitimate public procedure, it can be disclosed to the extent necessary.

4. Disclosures must be limited to the minimum necessary. Consult management when a decision is difficult.

Article 14 — Prohibitions

1. Builder does not violate these rules and MJX common rules and other related regulations.

2. The following buildings, displays, and activities are prohibited:

• Unreasonably obstructing another person's buildings, land, equipment, or activities • Seriously interfering with the server or participants’ activities • Facilities or activities prohibited by the common rules • Activities that management, for specific stated reasons, deems markedly inconsistent with MJX’s operational purposes or building environment • Activities primarily intended to harass, threaten, insult, exclude, or attack particular users

3. In determining whether something is inappropriate, management must consider the work’s content, location, purpose, effects on the surroundings, and relevant regulations together.

4. A design must not be prohibited solely because it is unusual or does not match an administrator’s personal preferences.

5. To ensure consistency, management records permitted and refused cases and decision criteria and publishes them when necessary.

6. Restrictions on religious facilities must be consistent with the common and related regulations, and their scope and reasons must be stated. Any MJX-specific restriction must have its authority and basis confirmed.

7. Except in emergencies, management should verify the facts and seek explanations from the parties concerned wherever possible.

8. Please do not judge that it is a violation because only the difference of opinion and reasonable operational criticism.

Chapter 4 — Leave of Absence and Withdrawal

Article 15 — Check Activity Status

1. You can check Builder’s activity status and management targets for purposes such as maintaining qualifications and privileges, transferring in charge, and solving operational problems.

2. When confirming, specify the purpose and response deadline as much as possible.

3. A Builder’s qualification must not be revoked immediately solely because the Builder did not respond.

4. If contact cannot be established for an extended period and operational difficulties arise, management may respond according to Articles 16 and 17.

Article 16 — Leave of Absence

1. A Builder may apply to management for leave when there are reasonable grounds, stating the reason and anticipated duration where possible.

2. Temporarily exempt from normal activity obligations during the approved leave period. The handling of rights, priority ownership rights, and assignments in charge is subject to this provision.

3. In the case of confirming permissions and responsive matters during the leave, in principle, a response period of 7 days or more is set from the notification. However, if there is a reasonable situation such as emergency, you can adjust.

4. If there is no response by the due date, the operation will strive for additional communication and confirmation of circumstances, and will not immediately cancel the work or qualification.

5. If neither substantive activity nor communication can be confirmed for at least 60 days, as a rule, management may review whether to maintain the Builder’s permissions and assigned projects.

6. The review must consider leave requests, advance notice, the person’s circumstances, and the status of projects. Where leave has been approved or reasonable circumstances exist, inactivity alone must not determine the outcome.

7. Even if you have regular contacts, you can make necessary confirmation according to the situation of the management and the impact of the development.

8. Restrictions on the number of times, period, and re-application intervals shall be determined separately if necessary.

Article 17 — Long-Term Suspension of Railway Lines

1. In principle, if substantial activities such as construction, maintenance, and operation of the route are not confirmed more than 60 days, the operation can confirm the status of the route and future plans.

2. A completed line that is operating and being managed normally must not be deemed inactive solely because no construction is underway.

3. If there are reasonable circumstances such as leave, preliminary declaration, technical problems, consider them.

4. Management may request an explanation or plans from the owner, but must not immediately revoke rights or forcibly remove a line solely because activity has stopped.

5. If the owner is no longer an active Builder, management should consider continued management, transfer, or joint management in light of the owner’s circumstances, existing rights, the line’s public importance, and the need for handover.

6. If the transfer destination is not determined by consultation, the operation can be determined in a reasonable manner such as recruitment, adjustment, lottery of the applicant.

7. In principle, prior notice and explanation are provided when changing or canceling the rights.

8. In a safety or public emergency, necessary measures such as temporary changes of management may be taken.

Article 18 — Handling of withdrawal and registration data

1. A Builder who withdraws must notify management.

2. Authorities, assignments, priority ownership rights, Schematic, resource packs, etc. shall be processed in with these provisions and related procedures.

3. As a guideline, management proceeds with necessary checks and procedures within 14 days of notification.

4. If you are unable to contact or cooperate with you, you can set up a reasonable period and decide to respond after trying to contact you.

5. Registered Schematics are governed by Article 9. Withdrawal alone does not automatically terminate existing licenses.

6. Data such as resource packs, models, textures, and sounds are subject to the terms and conditions of use with the creators and rights holders.

7. When registering or providing resource packs or similar data, the scope of permission for use, modification, redistribution, and continued use after withdrawal must be clarified.

8. Participation in or withdrawal from MJX does not, by itself, authorize continued use of data without the rights holder’s permission.

If the rights holder requests that use cease, management must review existing license terms, third-party rights, and effects on installed facilities before responding.

9. There is no automatic removal of existing buildings and routes due to only the withdrawal.

10. If there is a reasonable reason such as infringement of rights or serious violation of regulations, the operation can take necessary measures.

Chapter 5 — Disciplinary Measures and Dispute Resolution

Article 19 — Disciplinary Measures and Procedures

1. Operations can determine the necessary measures in consideration of violations, impacts, intentional and negligence, past violations, improvement status, etc.

2. Notes, warnings, improvement requests, equipment modification requests, item/coin collection, pause permissions, revocation of Builder qualifications, etc.

3. In principle, notify the contents of violations before serious disposal such as suspension of authority or cancellation of qualifications, and set up an opportunity to explain.

4. In emergencies, such as protecting safety, preventing serious harm, or preserving evidence, management may impose temporary restrictions without providing an opportunity for explanation in advance. It must notify the affected person of the reasons as soon as reasonably possible and provide an opportunity to explain afterward.

5. In serious disposal, notify the person of the contents, reasons, and application rules.

6. As a rule, a person subject to discipline may appeal to the designated contact point within 14 days after notification. If reasonable circumstances prevented a timely appeal, management may consider accepting a late appeal.

7. The disciplinary notice must specify how and when to appeal. An appellant must identify the measure challenged and the reasons for the appeal.

8. In the event of an objection, we will reconfirm the basis of disposal, facts, description of the person, and related regulations, and determine the maintenance, change, and cancellation.

9. In the re-exa , the person in charge who is not directly involved in the initial disposal decision will be examined. If it is difficult due to personnel, etc., consider how to ensure neutrality.

10. Unfavorable treatment solely because a person has lodged an appeal is prohibited.

11. Disposal will not be automatically stopped only by objection. However, the operation can decide to pause, change or cancel the disposal if necessary.

12. Restrictions needed for emergency safety may remain in place during review, but their necessity must be reassessed as appropriate, and they must be lifted or changed when no longer necessary.

13. MJX is also applied to the objection system of Article 12 of the Common Regulations, and if the procedure or authority is different, follow the priority of Article 22.

14. The implementation, recording and final judgment of disposal shall be in with common provisions and other relevant provisions.

Article 20 — Dispute Resolution Between Builders

1. If a dispute a s between Builder, such as land, buildings, routes, Schematic, and business, we first confirm the facts among the parties and strive to resolve them through consultation.

2. If it is not possible to solve it, it is possible to ask for arbitration and judgment to the operation.

3. The operation is determined by confirming both explanations, evidence, related regulations, and past circumstances.

4. In the event of serious disturbances, infringement of rights, or damage to the server, we can take necessary measures without waiting for consultation.

5. Any objection to the management decision is subject to Article 19 and the common provisions.

Article 21 — Joint Development

1. When multiple Builders develop together, they should agree in advance, wherever possible, on their respective tasks, management duties, permitted uses of deliverables, and creator attribution.

2. The right of the outcome is subject to the agreement between the parties, the rights and relevant provisions of each creator.

3. It is not possible to use or change the outcomes of other participants for reasons that there is no agreement.

4. In the event of a dispute concerning rights or management, follow Article 20.

5. Management may request development records or details of responsibility assignments when necessary.

Chapter 6 — Supplementary Provisions

Article 22 — Relationship with the Common Regulations and Amendment of These Rules

1. Matters not specified in this provision shall be subject to the MJX Common Provisions and other related provisions.

2. In a conflict, the common regulations generally take precedence, unless they expressly permit special provisions in individual regulations.

3. MGSA's common system is limited to the extent that MJX can change its own.

4. Operations may be revised as necessary. As a general rule, the change contents and the application start date are expressed in advance.

5. When existing Builders’ rights or activities are significantly affected, transitional measures may be established as necessary.

6. The detailed rules and operating standards shall be set to the extent not contrary to the provisions and the above.

7. Questions of interpretation and contradictions between provisions must be decided in light of related regulations, the intent of these Regulations, and MJX’s operational purposes.

8. The final decision-making of the entire MJX is in with the highest management regulations.

Supplementary Provisions

1. This provision shall be enforced from the date specified by the operation.

2. Builder qualifications, priority ownership rights, registered Schematic and existing buildings and routes are not revoked as a rule only for enforcement.

3. A reasonable transition period can be set for new applications, registrations, and displays.

4. If existing practices differ from these Regulations, management must inform the affected Builders of the changes and make individual adjustments when necessary.

5. The Schematic and resource pack license conditions registered before the enforcement of these Terms shall be reviewed and reaffirmed as necessary. The scope of licensing will not be extended automatically due to only enforcement.

6. Management may separately establish detailed rules, such as specific operational standards and lists of prohibited or permitted equipment.

MTR Japan Nexus Resource Pack

Revisions approved: November 1, 2026 / Effective date: to be announced separately

Operator: MTR Japan Nexus (MJX)

Preamble

These Regulations establish rules for proper management and use of official resource packs within MTR Japan Nexus (MJX) and for protecting their creators’ rights.

MJX respects creative activities and deliverables by participants and strives to maintain a safe and continuous resource pack environment.

This Agreement shall be used in conjunction with the MJX Common Server Regulations and other related regulations.

Article 1 — Scope and Definitions

1. This Agreement applies to the resource packs managed and distributed by the MJX operation and the models, textures, sounds, configuration files and other configuration data included in it.

2. Each title and term in these Terms shall be as follows:

◦ Administrator: A person responsible for servers, resource packs, or other operations under MJX regulations and assigned permissions.

◦ Builder: A person authorized to perform urban development, construction, and railway or transport infrastructure work through MGSA’s selection process or procedures recognized by MJX.

◦ Visitor: A general participant in MJX who belongs to the participation category defined in the relevant MJX regulations.

◦ Creator: A person who created models, textures, sounds, or other data included in a resource pack.

◦ Rights holder: A person who holds copyright or other authority to grant permission to use the data.

3. "Official Resource Pack" in this Agreement is a resource pack provided by the MJX operation.

4. As a general rule, use permissions set forth in these Terms shall be limited to the Minecraft server of MJX.

5. The resource pack used in the former MTR Japan Server (MJaS) is not automatically included in the MJX official resource pack according to these Terms.

Article 2 — Available actions

1. MJX participants may use the official resource pack according to their role and the rights holders’ terms of use.

2. Builder can use a resource pack that is allowed to use for urban development, railway construction, traffic facility maintenance and other recognized activities within MJX.

3. Visitors may use the official resource pack to display MJX vehicles, buildings, equipment, and other objects correctly and to participate in permitted in-game activities.

4. Normal downloading and loading by the Minecraft client, and creation of necessary caches, are permitted under these Regulations. This does not permit free redistribution or modification.

5. In the event that additional terms of use are determined for certain vehicles, models, etc., it is necessary to comply with the terms of use.

6. Publishing screenshots or videos made using the resource pack is subject to rules separately established by MJX and the rights holders’ terms of use.

7. This Article does not permit the use of third-party copyrights beyond the scope of permission of the right holder.

Article 3 — Prohibitions

3-1 Common Prohibitions

All users are not allowed to:

1. Redistributing all or part of the official resource pack to third parties without the necessary authority or permission.

2. Acts of using official resource packs without permission in Minecraft servers, single-play world and other environments other than MJX.

3. Extracting, modifying, porting, or repurposing vehicles, models, textures, sounds, or other data from the resource pack without permission.

4. Acts that acquire or publish official resource packs without permission except the normal method specified by the operation.

5. Acts of illegally deleting or altering the author's name, right display, and usage conditions.

6. Acts of harassing, discriminating, threatening or other annoying to other users using a resource pack.

7. Acts of using resource packs for the purpose of contrary to the MJX common server provisions, such as political and religious advertising.

8. Acts that violate laws, public order and morals, MJX common server regulations or other related regulations.

9. Any other act that MJX operation deems inappropriate based on specific reasons.

However, if there is a valid license of the right holder or if it is legally permitted, it will be handled in that case.

3-2 Prohibited Conduct by Builders

In addition to the preceding paragraph, Builders must not engage in the following acts:

1. Installing vehicles or models made by other creators on one’s own railway lines or facilities in violation of their permitted terms of use.

2. Acts of remodeling, re-registration, and re buting the work of others without the necessary consent.

3. Acts of providing data in the resource pack for paid sales, commercialization and other commercial secondary use without the consent of the right holder.

4. Using a vehicle in accident depictions without permission from that vehicle’s original creator.

5. Misrepresenting oneself as the creator or legitimate rights holder of someone else’s work.

3-3 Depictions of Vehicle Accidents

1. In the event that the vehicle included in the above resource pack is used to express an accident, in principle, it is necessary to obtain prior consent from the creator of the vehicle.

2. If the creator is prohibited to express an accident, do not use the vehicle for an accident.

3. If the license of the creator cannot be confirmed, it will be handled as unlicensed.

4. The approval of the planning and event by MJX is not a substitute for the permission of the creator.

5. If multiple rights holders are involved, such as joint production, they must obtain the consent of the necessary rights holder.

6. Even if the creator himself/herself has granted permission, he/ must comply with the MJX Common Server Regulations and other related regulations.

3-4 Modifications and Permissions

1. In principle, the license of the right holder should be obtained when remodeling vehicles and models of other creators.

2. If the remodeling status or conditions are expressed for each work, follow the conditions.

3. The licensing should be made in a way that can be confirmed between the parties regarding the target data, purpose of use, content of modification, and whether or not of re bution.

4. It is not to have been allowed to re bute, use externally, commercial secondary use of data itself, or any other action only with the permission of modification.

Article 4 — Protection of creators and rights

1. The copyright of the creator or the right holder is not transferred to the MJX operation because only the data is provided to the Official Resource Pack.

2. When works are registered or included, the necessary permission of the rights holders and the applicable terms of use must be confirmed.

3. Approval of registration and recording by MJX operation is not the transfer of the rights held by the right holder or the use beyond the scope of permission.

4. The creator can specify conditions such as use, modification, and reuse by other Builder within the scope permitted by themselves.

5. Respect the agreement between the relevant rights holders and the rights of third parties for joint production.

6. In the event of any claims, cancellations, or other claims from the right holder, the MJX operation will respond after confirming the existing license conditions, usage status and third party rights.

7. Copyrights and other rights are not automatically extinguished for reasons only to unsubscribe or withdraw from MJX.

8. This Article does not interfere with the handling of production and registration data set forth in the MJX Builder provisions and other related regulations.

Article 5 — Distribution and Re-Distribution

1. As a rule, distribution and updates of the official resource pack are carried out by MJX management or persons properly authorized to do so.

2. MJX can temporarily stop distribution, update or use of resource packs in the following cases:

◦ When serious defects or defects of data are found.

◦ When copyright or other rights-related problems are confirmed.

◦ In the event of unauthorized use, re bution, or other violations of regulations.

◦ In the event of a security problem or a serious problem in server operation.

◦ In addition, if a stop is 、 necessary for reasonable reasons.

3. If distribution is suspended, management will publicize the reasons, effects, and anticipated resumption to the extent possible.

4. In case of emergency, you can stop without advance notice. However, if necessary, it will be explained.

5. Suspension of distribution does not extinguish the rights held by rights holders.

Article 6 — Response to violations

1. In the event of a violation of these Terms, the MJX operation can take necessary measures to consider the contents, impacts, intentional and negligence of the violation, past violations, etc.

2. Measures may include the following:

◦ Attention or warning.

◦ Suspension of use of the relevant data.

◦ Restrictions on resource packs.

◦ Pause of related activity rights.

◦ Restrictions on access to the Minecraft server or other MJX-related services.

◦ Suspension of server access or a ban in serious cases.

3. In the event of any unauthorised re bution or infringement of rights, MJX operation can cooperate with the right holder and request to delete or stop the relevant service operators, etc.

4. The claims in the、 paragraph shall be based on the necessary rights or justifiable grounds and shall not mean that the MJX operation has the authority to stop or delete external services directly.

5. Any violation or disposition related to MGSA shall be governed by the provisions of Article 7 and other relevant provisions of the MJX Common Server.

6. As a rule, reasons for disciplinary action must be communicated. Temporary measures may be taken first where needed to protect safety or preserve evidence.

Article 7 — Appeals

1. The User may request an explanation in the MJX operation for the disposal based on these Terms and Conditions and make an objection.

2. The method and examination of objection is described in Article 12 and other related regulations of the MJX Common Server.

3. Where a disciplinary measure against a Builder is subject to Article 19 of the Builder Regulations, the procedures under that article also apply.

4. The operation can confirm the contents of the request and change, reduce, cancel, etc. of disposal if necessary.

5. Users must not be subjected to unfavorable measures solely because they filed an appeal.

6. The effectiveness of measures during an appeal is governed by related regulations. Management may suspend, modify, or lift a measure when necessary.

7. MGSA-wide BAN, etc., if MJX cannot be changed independently, follow the relevant system and authority.

Article 8 — Disclaimer

1. The MJX operation does not guarantee that the official resource pack will work properly in all environments and will continue to provide.

2. Distribution, updates, or use of resource packs may be temporarily restricted due to system failures, specification changes, communication outages, or other circumstances.

3. The User shall comply with these Terms and Conditions when using the Resource Pack.

4. MJX management’s responsibilities are governed by applicable laws. These Regulations do not exempt any liability that cannot legally be excluded or limited.

Article 9 — Information handling

1. The privacy policy currently in effect within MJX applies to user information collected in connection with resource-pack distribution, authentication, misuse prevention, or maintenance.

2. We will comply with the privacy policy for the types, purpose of use, retention period, and provision of information to be acquired.

3. This Agreement does not allow the acquisition or use of personal information not described in the Privacy Policy.

Article 10 — Related Regulations and Amendments

1. The MJX Common Server Rules, MJX Builder Regulations and other related regulations are provided for matters not specified in these Terms.

2. In the event of any interpre differences between these Terms and other provisions, the Company shall consider the applicable activities, rights and representations of each provision, and shall be in。 with the MJX Common Server Regulations and other relevant provisions.

3. Under no circumstances do these Regulations automatically extinguish rights granted by law or rights held by legitimate rights holders.

4. MJX management may revise these Regulations in response to changes in the system, operating environment, rights, laws, or other relevant circumstances.

5. As a rule, the contents and effective dates of revisions will be announced in advance. In emergencies, necessary measures may be taken first and announced as soon as possible afterward.

6. The scope of use by existing creators and rights holders will not be extended automatically due to revision of the terms.

Supplementary Provisions

1. These Regulations take effect on the effective date formally approved and announced by MJX management.

2. These Terms apply to the resource pack that MJX officially manages and distributes.

3. Resource packs and their data in the former MJaS era are not automatically transferred or recorded to MJX by the enforcement of these Terms.

4. Before enactment, the license, the rights and registration conditions of the current MJX official resource pack will not be revoked for the reason only for revision.

5. MJX management may separately establish and announce detailed procedures for resource-pack delivery, lists of permitted uses, registrations, and similar matters when necessary.

1. MTR Japan Nexus (MJX) may collect and store information about users in providing and managing its Minecraft server and related services.

3. This policy applies to handling information described in this policy in connection with MJX-operated Minecraft servers, resource-pack management systems, and related services.

4. In the case of newly obtaining information not described in this policy in the above service, we will clearly state the information, purpose of use, and other necessary matters in advance and make revisions or individual announcements of this policy.

Article 1 — Information Collected

1-1 Information obtained based on user consent

MJX may collect and store the following information when a user is given the necessary explanation and consents to use a feature that requires it.

• Minecraft Account UUID

If the user does not agree to the acquisition of information, some functions that require such information may not be available.

1-2 Information to be acquired by service operation

In order to ensure the operation, maintenance and security of the resource pack management system, MJX may acquire and store the following information to the extent necessary, regardless of whether or not the user consents when accessing the system.

• Date and time of access

• Version of the resource-pack management Mod used

These information will be handled within the scope of the purposes set forth in this Policy.

1-3 Principles for Acquisition of Information

1. MJX collects information to the extent necessary to achieve the purpose of use.

2. We will not acquire information by false or other illegal means.

3. If the information other than the information described in this article is obtained, the contents and purpose of use will be notified or announced in an appropriate manner.

Article 2 — Purpose of Use of Information

MJX uses the acquired information for the following purposes:

1. Preventing unauthorized use and redistribution of resource packs

2. Managing access logs and investigating unauthorized access

3. Investigation and troubleshooting of system failures, defects, and related problems

4. Improvement of resource packs and related systems

5. Analyze usage and provide stable services

6. Security measures and maintaining the safety of the Services

7. Other administrative tasks related to the above purposes

As a rule, MJX will obtain the person’s prior consent before handling personal information beyond what is necessary for the stated purposes, except where required or otherwise permitted by law.

MTR Japan Nexus — Economic Regulations

Established October 5, 2026

MTR Japan Nexus

Chapter 1 — General Provisions

Article 1 — Purpose

These Regulations establish the necessary rules for economic activity within MTR Japan Nexus (MJX), provide participants with opportunities to live, work, consume, and conduct businesses, and aim to support a healthy and sustainable economy.

Article 2 — Fundamental Principles

1. MJX’s economic activities are based on the ability of participants to freely provide products, services and labor.

2. MJX provides the necessary public services so participants can maintain a minimum standard of living and activity.

3. Public services aim to provide a stable supply of goods and services essential to participants; they do not aim to offer premium quality, speed, frequency, convenience, or other added value.

4. Private operators can provide their own services based on public services by quality, speed, frequency, convenience, equipment, value-added and other elements.

5. Administrators may establish or change prices, wages, and other economic conditions as needed to ensure the stability and continuity of MJX’s economy.

Article 3 — Definitions

In this provision, the following terms have the following meanings:

1. "Administrator": A person who has authority as an administrator of MJX.

2. “Supreme Administrator”: The person holding the highest administrative authority in MJX.

3. "Participants": All who join MJX.

4. “Visitor”: A person who participates in everyday urban life, economic activities, and other general activities within MJX.

5. Builder: People who are mainly engaged in the development and construction of MJX cities, buildings, transportation facilities, and more.

6. "Business operators": A person who continuously or repetitively provides products or services at MJX.

7. "Public Business": A business in which an administrator provides minimum necessary products or services to MJX participants.

8. "Private Business": A business that administrators and other participants do as their own business and does not fall under the public business.

9. “Employment”: An activity in which a person provides time or labor under an operator’s instructions or supervision in exchange for wages.

10. “Construction Contract”: A contract under which a person receives remuneration to provide completed buildings, facilities, or other deliverables.

Chapter 2 — Builders and Visitors

Article 4 — Builder Activities

1. A Builder’s primary role is urban development and construction within MJX.

2. As a rule, the product-purchase, wage, housing, employment, and other economic restrictions in these Regulations do not apply to Builders’ ordinary urban-development and construction activities.

3. Builders may use Creative mode or other environments authorized by management to the extent needed for urban development.

4. Building materials, tools and other resources required for ordinary urban development and construction activities by the builder are not subject to economic transactions as a rule.

Article 5 — Builder's Economic Activities

1. Even Builders are subject to these Regulations when they operate stores, sell goods, provide services, hire workers, or conduct other economic activities.

2. In principle, when the builder provides his own architectural technology or work to others and receives compensation for finished products and other outcomes, he treats it as an architectural contract.

3. As a rule, provisions on wages and working hours for ordinary employment do not apply to construction contracts.

4. It is not exempted from obligations when performing economic activities only because it is a builder.

5. Notwithstanding the preceding paragraphs, if a construction contract in practice consists of hourly labor under the operator’s instructions or management, it may be treated as ordinary employment.

Article 6 — Visitor Activities

1. Visitors can engage in labor, business, consumption, residence and other economic activities at MJX.

2. This policy applies to ordinary economic activities by visitors.

3. When a visitor conducts urban development or architectural activities, it is possible to acquire necessary materials and other goods through public or private business.

Chapter 3 — Currency and initial funds

Article 7 — Currency

1. In principle, "G" is used for economic activities in MJX.

2. G is used as a consideration for goods, services, labor and other economic activities within MJX.

3. The issuance and distribution of G and other activities affecting the money supply are managed by administrators as a rule.

Article 8 — G issuance and management

1. Administrators may issue or distribute G for public services, rewards, compensation, initial allowances, or other MJX operational needs.

2. Administrators can adjust the issuance as needed to prevent inflation or other economic problems caused by excessive G issuance.

3. When administrators issue new G in quantities that significantly affect the economy, they must record the reasons and purposes.

Article 9 — Initial Allowance

1. As a general rule, the following will be paid to the visitor who participated in MJX.

• Bread: 1 stack (64 items) — 2,000G

2. Initial payments can be changed due to economic conditions and other circumstances.

3. Administrators may decide not to provide Builders with the normal initial allowance.

Chapter 4 — Public Services and Pricing

Article 10 — Public Services

1. Administrators may provide public services supplying goods and services necessary for participants’ minimum standard of living and activity within MJX.

2. Public services aim to ensure the minimum standard of living and activity.

3. Public services are not intended to maximize profits or surpass private businesses’ service levels.

4. The scope of the public business shall be determined as necessary by the administrator.

5. Public businesses do not interfere with simplicity or limited services in terms of quality, speed, frequency, convenience or other aspects to achieve minimal objectives.

Article 11 — Public Prices

1. Administrators may set public prices for products or services provided as public businesses.

2. The public price shall be the standard price for maintaining the minimum level of service required in MJX for such products or services.

3. The public price can be changed depending on the economic situation, the provision cost, usage status and other circumstances.

4. As a rule, private operators must not offer goods or services for which a public price has been set at a price below that public price.

5. Notwithstanding the。 paragraph, this is not the case where the administrator is specifically permitted.

Article 12 — Food Products

1. The price of bread provided as a public business is 150G per 6 pieces.

2. Administrators may change the price of the paragraph according to economic conditions and other circumstances.

3. Where management sets a public price for food items other than bread, the preceding article applies.

4. For groceries that the administrator does not set public prices, the operator can freely set prices.

Article 13 — Prices Charged by Private Businesses

1. Private operators may freely set prices for goods and services subject to public prices, provided the prices are at or above the public price.

2. In addition to price, private companies can differentiate from public businesses by quality, speed, frequency, convenience, facilities, location, value-added and other factors.

3. Even if a private company provides goods or services identical or equivalent to a public business, it is not obliged to provide the same quality, speed, frequency or convenience as a public business.

Chapter 5 — Housing

Article 14 — Public housing

1. Administrators can provide public housing to ensure minimum living environment for participants.

2. In principle, the rent of public housing is 10,000G per month.

3. Public housing facilities and services are based on the minimum requirements for residence.

4. Public housing is not intended to provide luxury, comfort and other added value.

Article 15 — Acquisition of Public Housing

1. For public housing, if the tenant pays rent for 12 months, it is possible to set up a system that can acquire the housing.

2. When implementing the acquisition system of the。 paragraph, the administrator shall separately determine the ownership, land handling, reconstruction, transfer, sale and other necessary conditions.

3. Administrators may modify or suspend the acquisition scheme in response to economic conditions or changes to the housing system.

Article 16 — Privately Operated Housing

1. Private operators can provide housing and other buildings.

2. The price and contract conditions of private housing shall be determined by the operator within the scope not contrary to the provisions of MJX.

3. For private housing, you can freely set price, size, facility, location and other value-added.

4. If a public price has been set for privately operated housing, Articles 11 and 13 apply.

Chapter 6 — Transportation

Article 17 — Public Transportation

1. Administrators can provide public transportation as a minimum means of transportation for participants to travel within MJX.

2. Public transportation is intended to ensure the lowest possible means of transportation that can be supplied to ward offices and other major public facilities.

3. Public transportation is not necessarily intended to provide high speed, frequency of operation, convenience, comfort and other added value.

4. Public transportation may operate simply or inefficiently as long as it fulfills its minimum objectives.

Article 18 — Public Transportation Fares

1. As a rule, the minimum fare for public transportation is 100G.

2. Administrators may set fares according to routes, distance, usage, operating conditions, and other relevant circumstances.

3. Administrators may change fares according to economic and transportation conditions.

Article 19 — Privately Operated Transportation

1. Private operators can provide transportation services separately from public transportation.

2. The price of private transportation can be determined by the operator to the extent that it is not contrary to the price conditions specified by the administrator.

Article 19 — Public Transport Operations

Chapter 7 — Construction Materials and Tools

Article 20 — Building Materials

1. Administrators can sell basic building materials required for construction activities to visitors and other participants.

2. As a rule, basic building materials are sold in one stack (64 pieces).

3. The sales price shall be determined by the administrator considering the economic situation and other circumstances.

4. The general urban development and architectural activities of the builder do not require the purchase of materials in this article.

Article 21 — Tools

1. When Visitors join MJX, administrators may provide basic tools needed for activities free of charge.

2. Higher-performance tools, specialized tools, and additional equipment may be offered for a fee.

3. Free provision of ordinary tools is not necessary for Builders using Creative mode or similar environments.

4. Administrators can change the type of tools provided free of charge in consideration of game balance and economic situation.

Chapter 8 — Wages and Construction Contracts

Article 22 — Wage Basics

1. As a rule, anyone performing work in MJX must be paid wages corresponding to that work.

2. Wages must be paid in G.

Article 23 — Construction Contracts

1. Builders and other participants may undertake paid contracts to provide completed buildings, facilities, or other deliverables.

2. Remuneration, deliverables, delivery dates, specifications and other conditions in architectural contracts shall be agreed in advance between parties.

3. As a rule, building contracts are distinguished from ordinary labor.

4. The minimum wage in Article 24 and the maximum wage in Article 25 do not directly apply to remuneration for construction contracts.

5. In the event that the actual condition of architectural contracts is carried out by hourly labor under the instructions or management of the operator, it can be treated as normal labor.

Article 24 — Minimum Wage

As a rule, wages for work must not be less than 1,000G per hour.

Article 25 — Maximum Wage

1. As a rule, wages for work must not exceed 10,000G per hour.

2. The limit in the preceding paragraph is intended to prevent excessive currency inflow and economic instability within MJX.

3. The upper limit of paragraph 1 can be changed temporarily if the administrator admits special circumstances.

Article 26 — Wage Calculation

1. As a rule, wages are calculated in 10-minute units.

2. 1,000G labor per hour is based on about 167G per 10 minutes.

3. Employers must specify the wage offered when recruiting, with amounts that can be calculated in 10-minute units.

Chapter 9 — Employment

Article 27 — Disclosure of Employment Conditions

When a company hires a worker, it must clarify the following matters at the time of the job.

1. Terms of employment

2. Wages

3. Duties to be performed

4. Other conditions required

Article 28 — Number of Employees

1. As a general rule, up to 3 workers can be employed at the same time unless otherwise specified by the administrator.

2. Administrators may change the worker limit in the preceding paragraph in consideration of business scale, usage, public interest, and other circumstances.

3. Employers must not prevent workers from working for other employers.

4. Employers must not force workers to join their business or work exclusively for them.

Article 29 — Working Hours

1. As a rule, a single work session must last at least 10 minutes and no more than 3 hours.

2. If an administrator considers it necessary, workers may be employed beyond the limit in the preceding paragraph.

3. In the case of long-term work, the operator shall clearly indicate that at the time of the job.

Article 30 — Payment of Wages

1. Employers must pay wages within three days after the work ends.

2. When a worker reports unpaid wages, an administrator may investigate the facts as necessary.

3. If unpaid wages are confirmed, an administrator may advance the unpaid amount on the employer’s behalf when necessary.

4. In the circumstances described in the preceding paragraph, the employer who failed to pay wages generally may not hire new workers for 30 days.

Article 31 — Employment Records

1. When hiring workers, employers must record the following details:

• Employer name • Worker name • Work description • Working hours • Wages

2. Employment records generally do not have to be made public.

3. If an administrator requests employment records, the employer must submit them within three days.

4. Administrators may generally restrict employers who fail to create employment records or record required details from hiring new workers for 14 days.
