Seven Official Regulations
Status and Summary

An English guide to the seven MJX regulations, based on the 50-page document set approved November 1, 2026. For authoritative wording, use the Japanese original PDFs.

Approval is not the same as taking effect

The amendments to 04 (Resource Pack), 05 (Privacy Policy), and 07 (Visitor Rules) were approved on November 1, 2026. However, their effective dates have not been specified. Do not assume they replaced the previous active provisions immediately.

This page is an English overview, not an official translation or replacement for the original regulations. Use “Read original PDF” on each card to see the complete Japanese text. In case of differences, refer to the Japanese original and official MJX announcements.

Cinema System Usage Policy

English summary · Japanese original prevails

Sets rules for copyright, permissions, prohibited uses, and administration of audiovisual content in the MJX cinema system.

Enacted October 11, 20268 articlesUpdated set pages 1–4
Read original PDF ↗Japanese original / 4 pages · opens in a new tabEnglish summary PDF ↗
Read summary ＋

Articles 1–2 | Scope and permitted content

Eligible content includes works created with the necessary rights, material authorized by rights holders, works whose licensing permits exhibition, and content approved by MJX management.

Articles 3–4 | Prohibited uses and YouTube

Unauthorized screening of movies, anime, or paid streaming content, use of illicit uploads, and circumvention of access or copy protection are prohibited. A video being publicly accessible on YouTube does not by itself grant permission to screen or rebroadcast it in MJX.

Articles 5–8 | Administration, enforcement, and changes

Maintenance and special events are also subject to applicable laws and terms. Violations may lead to playback suspension or restrictions. Liability that cannot legally be excluded is not waived.

Resource Pack Regulations

English summary · Japanese original prevails

An approved revision clarifying use, modification, redistribution, and protection of creators and rights holders for official resource packs.

Revision approved November 1, 2026Preamble, 10 articles, supplementary provisionsUpdated set pages 21–28
Read original PDF ↗Japanese original / 8 pages · opens in a new tabEnglish summary PDF ↗
Read summary ＋

Articles 1–2 | Scope and authorized use

Applies to data officially managed and distributed. Ordinary downloads and caching do not grant permission for redistribution or outside use. Older MJaS material is not automatically transferred under this regulation.

Article 3 | Prohibited uses and accident depictions

Unauthorized redistribution, extraction, modification, and external use are prohibited. As a general rule, depicting accidents involving a vehicle requires prior permission from that vehicle's creator; event approval from management does not replace this consent.

Articles 4–5 | Creators' rights and distribution management

Providing a work does not automatically transfer copyright to the server management. Requests to stop use or unregister a work are considered alongside earlier license conditions and third-party rights.

Articles 6–10 | Violations, appeals, information, and amendment

Covers proportionate restrictions, appeals, and privacy provisions. Amending the regulation does not automatically expand any existing permission or license.

Effective-date note

The revision has been approved, but the effective date will be announced separately.

Privacy Policy

English summary · Japanese original prevails

An approved revision outlining data collection, purposes, retention, third-party sharing, user requests, and security safeguards.

Revision approved November 1, 2026Articles 0–9 and supplementary provisionsUpdated set pages 29–34
Read original PDF ↗Japanese original / 6 pages · opens in a new tabEnglish summary PDF ↗
Read summary ＋

Articles 0–1 | Information collected

With consent, the server may collect a Minecraft account UUID. For resource-pack administration, access timestamps and the management mod version may be collected where needed.

Articles 2–3 | Use and retention

Data may be used to prevent misuse, investigate failures, and improve systems. The stated retention period for user information generally extends up to six months after leaving MJX, with deletion promptly thereafter.

Articles 4–7 | Sharing, cookies, user requests, and security

Sharing personal data generally requires consent, subject to legal exceptions. Users may request access, correction, suspension of use, or deletion of their information.

Articles 8–9 and supplementary provisions | Contact and amendments

Contact MJX management (representative: warudora), Discord channel <#1320245263796801588>. The amended policy has no stated effective date; the prior active policy remains applicable until then.

Economic Regulations

Framework for in-game currency G, public and private services, labor, employment, transactions, economic management, and administrative powers.

Enacted October 5, 202615 chapters, 47 articlesUpdated set pages 35–52

Chapters 1–2 | Foundations and participant roles

Distinguishes baseline public services from competitive private businesses. Ordinary Builder development is generally exempt from economic restrictions, while business operations remain regulated.

Chapters 3–8 | Currency and public services

Initial allocations, earning and spending currency, transport and everyday services, and public pricing. Consult the original regulation and current management announcements for specific conditions.

Chapter 9 | Employment (Articles 27–31)

Employment is generally limited to three people. A work session normally lasts at least 10 minutes and no more than three hours. Wages are to be paid within three days after the work ends.

Chapters 10–15 | Trade, oversight, misconduct, and amendments

Peer-to-peer transactions, fair markets, managers' private businesses and conflicts of interest, economic adjustments, senior management authority, fraudulent transactions, and amendments.

Visitor Rules

English summary · Japanese original prevails

An approved revision covering Visitor participation in Survival Mode and block-editing permissions managed by the protection mod.

Revision approved November 1, 2026Participation, work, and spendingUpdated set pages 53–55
Read original PDF ↗Japanese original / 3 pages · opens in a new tabEnglish summary PDF ↗
Read summary ＋

1 | Participation environment

Visitors generally join in Survival Mode. The MJX protection mod restricts block placement, destruction, and changes to city facilities unless management grants permissions in specified areas. An initial allowance is provided when joining.

2 | Ways to earn currency

Visitors may earn coins or points through customer service, driving, guiding, commissioned tasks, shops, selling products, vending machines, events, and competitions. The form of rewards varies by activity.

3 | Ways to spend

Possible uses include MTR fares and passes, road tolls, vehicles, mobility products, eligible equipment, furniture, appliances, food, and property purchase or rental. Some events may allow points to be converted into coins.

Effective-date note

These changes were approved on November 1, 2026, but no effective date has been specified. Earlier guidance should not be interpreted as implementing them immediately.

Which provisions currently apply?

Documents 01, 02, 03, and 06 remain in force without changes. Revisions 04, 05, and 07 are approved but have no specified effective date. In particular, the amended Privacy Policy states that the previous active policy remains applicable until the amendment takes effect.
